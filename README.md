## Santa Competition Problems

| Year | Competition | Major problem |
|---|---|---|
| [2012](./2012/README.md) | [Traveling Santa Problem](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/traveling-santa-problem) | Find two routes through the chimneys that share no edges, minimizing the length of the longer route. |
| [2013](./2013/README.md) | [Packing Santa's Sleigh](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/packing-santas-sleigh) | Pack 3D presents into a sleigh with a fixed footprint, keeping the arrangement as compact as possible while accounting for delivery order. |
| [2014](./2014/README.md) | [Helping Santa's Helpers](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/helping-santas-helpers) | Assign elves to toys and schedule their work to finish production efficiently. |
| [2015](./2015/README.md) | [Santa's Stolen Sleigh](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santas-stolen-sleigh) | Route sleighs to deliver gifts while minimizing weight-dependent reindeer travel cost and respecting sleigh capacity. |
| [2016](./2016/README.md) | [Santa's Uncertain Bags](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santas-uncertain-bags) | Pack gifts into bags to maximize total carried weight without exceeding each bag's limit or the minimum gift count. |
| [2017](./2017/README.md) | [Santa Gift Matching Challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-gift-matching) | Assign gifts to children to maximize child and Santa happiness, while keeping twins and triplets together. |
| [2018](./2018/README.md) | [Traveling Santa 2018 - Prime Paths](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/traveling-santa-2018-prime-paths) | Find a short route visiting every city while accounting for extra travel cost when the route does not leave a prime city every tenth step. |
| [2019](./2019/README.md) | [Santa's Workshop Tour 2019](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-workshop-tour-2019) | Assign families to workshop visit dates based on their preferences while respecting daily attendance constraints. |
| [2019](./2019/README.md) | [Santa 2019 - Revenge of the Accountants](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2019-revenge-of-the-accountants) | Schedule families for workshop visits while minimizing the additional accounting costs incurred by the assignment. |
| [2020](./2020/README.md) | [Santa 2020 - The Candy Cane Contest](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2020) | Choose among 100 changing-reward vending machines over 2,000 attempts to collect as many candy canes as possible. |
| [2021](./2021/README.md) | [Santa 2021 - The Merry Movie Montage](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2021) | Create short movie schedules for three groups that cover every required combination while respecting starting-movie and wildcard constraints. |
| [2022](./2022/README.md) | [Santa 2022 - The Christmas Card Conundrum](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2022) | Optimize a robotic arm's movement and print-color changes to produce a Christmas card efficiently. |
| [2023](./2023/README.md) | [Santa 2023 - The Polytope Permutation Puzzle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2023) | Solve varied cube-like geometric puzzles in as few moves as possible. |
| [2024](./2024/README.md) | [Santa 2024 - The Perplexity Permutation Puzzle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2024) | Restore scrambled words in Christmas stories to minimize passage perplexity. |
| [2025](./2025/README.md) | [Santa 2025 - Christmas Tree Packing Challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2025) | Pack Christmas-tree shapes into boxes as efficiently as possible, minimizing the space required. |
